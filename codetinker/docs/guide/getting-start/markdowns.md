## Heading
You can use regular Markdown headings.

**Example:**
```markdown
## Level 2 title

### Level 3 title

#### Level 4 title

##### Level 5 title

###### Level 6 title
```
Each Markdown heading will appear as a table of contents entry.

## Paragraph
